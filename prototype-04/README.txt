HOW TO POSITION HOTSPOTS
1. Enter Hospital, click "Place Hotspots" (upper left).
2. Choose "Walk to next camera" from the dropdown.
3. Click the FLOOR down the hallway where you want the glowing arrow.
4. Choose the door name, then click the correct door in that panorama.
5. Click "Done · Test Hotspots" to try them.
6. Use the bottom Forward/Back buttons to change camera positions, and
   repeat calibration for C2 and C3. For the return direction, TURN
   AROUND before placing "Walk toward entrance" on the floor.
7. Download marker positions (JSON) to preserve your work and share it.
   You can import that JSON in another browser or computer.

Markers are saved automatically in THIS browser using localStorage,
not to GitHub. They will not appear on Phillip's browser unless
we bake the exported JSON into the website in a subsequent update.
Send the exported JSON back to ChatGPT to create the final demo build.

The original Prototype 02 gallery design is preserved.
Camera images are the actual C1/C2/C3 KeyShot renders.
Zoom: 14 degrees / 270 ms. Dissolve begins after zoom; soft blur
is applied during the dissolve. Browser support varies.

NOTE: Marker locations are per panorama, not shared across scenes.
Navigation arrows do not yet rotate the view to face their destination.
Place each arrow in the actual direction you intend to travel.

If you don't see the "Place Hotspots" button, check you're at /prototype-04/.
